How we build

  • Traced, not imagined

    Every step maps to real functions, files and data structures in the system's own source code.

  • Explain the why

    Each step comes with a plain-English explanation of what is happening and why it matters.

  • Small and careful

    A few focused tools, built and maintained with care, rather than many half-finished ones.

About

Diafyx comes from the Greek dia, “through”, and phainein, “to show”: the same roots as diaphanous. Showing what happens through a system, from the request going in to the result coming out, is the whole idea.

Diafyx is an independent studio run by Sepuri Sai Krishna, a data engineer who spends a lot of time inside the internals of data and distributed systems.
